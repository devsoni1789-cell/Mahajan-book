How interest is calculated

Interest is simple interest on the outstanding principal, charged per calendar month. Every loan is charged at least 1 month's interest. The moment the date crosses into a new calendar month, a full extra month is charged — days are never charged as a fraction of a month. Partial payments first clear any interest due, then reduce the principal.
